Wet waste

Separate fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and garden cuttings. Keep plastic, glass, and liquids out of the compost container.

Dry waste

Rinse food residue from accepted containers, flatten cardboard, and keep paper dry. Check local rules for plastic films and cartons.

Hazardous waste

Keep chemicals, paint, and batteries in their original containers. Never pour them down a drain or place them in regular curbside waste.

E-waste

Use a certified electronics drop-off or collection event. Remove personal data from devices and keep loose batteries separate.

Composting

Balance moist food scraps with dry leaves or shredded paper, and turn the pile for airflow. Follow local guidance on meat and dairy.

Do’s & don’ts

Do sort by your local collection rules and secure bags against wind. Don’t mix sharp, hot, or hazardous material with household waste.
